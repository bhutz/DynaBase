Data sources

This file lists the data and data sources.

Published Sources

  • Quadratic polynomials z^2 + c
    • Over QQ
      • Bjorn Poonen. The classificiation of rational preperiodic points of quadratic polynomials over Q: a refined conjecture. Math. Z., 228(1):11--29, 1998
        • The examples contained in the main table
    • Quadratic Number Fields
      • John R. Doyle, Xander Faber, and David Krumm. Preperiodic points for quadratic polynomials over quadratic fields. New York J. Math., 20:507--605, 2014
        • The examples contained in the appendix
  • Cubic Polynomials
    • Over QQ
      • Robert Benedetto, Ben Dickman, Sasha Joseph, Ben Krause, Dan Rubin, and Xinwen Zhou. Computing points of small height for cubic polynomials. Involve, 2:37--64, 2009.
        • 103 distinct preperiodic graphs from https://mgolech.github.io/cubics/index.html
  • Polynomials of degree 2 through 15
    • Over QQ
      • John R. Doyle and Trevor Hyde. Polynomials with many rational preperiodic points. Trans. Amer. Math. Soc., 378(8):5981--6011, 2025. (arXiv:2201.11707)
        • Table 2 (degrees 2-9, given explicitly) and Table 4 (degrees 10-15, given by the values f(1), ..., f(d+1); recovered by Lagrange interpolation)
        • The degree 2 and 3 examples are conjugate to maps already included (Poonen's x^2 - 29/16 and Benedetto et al.'s II18), so they add no new functions; they attach this citation to those maps
  • Quadratic Rational Functions
    • Over QQ
      • Robert L. Benedetto, Ruqian Chen, Trevor Hyde, Yordanka Kovacheva, and Colin White. Small dynamical heights for quadratic polynomials and rational functions. Exp. Math., 23(4):433--447, 2014.
        • First 10 maps from each Period X, Length Y file at https://rlbenedetto.people.amherst.edu/quadratdata/ (122 maps; the maps only, not the orbits)
  • Polynomials of degree 2 through 13 and rational functions of degree 2 through 4
    • Over QQ
      • Benjamin Hutz. A genetic algorithm for generating extreme examples in arithmetic dynamics. arXiv:2601.11482, 2026.
        • The maps in the appendix (Extended Data Set) tables Many Preperiodic Points, Long Periodic Cycles, and Long Preperiodic Tail; the Small Height ratio tables are not included
        • Each map is recovered by interpolation from the orbit the paper lists for it (164 distinct maps: 125 polynomials, 39 rational functions)
  • Postcritically finite (PCF) maps over QQ - complete classifications, up to conjugacy over QQbar
    • Quadratic polynomials
      • Patrick Ingram. A finiteness result for post-critically finite polynomials. Int. Math. Res. Not. IMRN, 2012(3):524--543, 2012.
        • Section 4: z^2 + c is PCF for c in {0, -1, -2} only (these are already in the Poonen data; this adds the citation). Also the seven monic centered PCF cubics z^3 + Az + B (6 classes)
    • Cubic polynomials
      • Jacqueline Anderson, Michelle Manes, and Bella Tobin. Cubic post-critically finite polynomials defined over Q. ANTS XIV, Open Book Ser. 4:23--38, 2020.
        • All 15 classes (Theorem / Table 1)
    • Quartic polynomials
      • Benjamin Fraser. On PCF polynomials. PhD thesis, York University, 2024.
        • All 16 classes (Theorem 4.9, Table 4.1; bicritical classes in the Table 4.2 form)
    • Quadratic rational functions
      • David Lukas, Michelle Manes, and Diane Yap. A census of quadratic post-critically finite rational functions defined over Q. LMS J. Comput. Math., 17(A):314--329, 2014.
        • All 12 classes (Theorem 1), including the 3 polynomials
    • No complete classification of degree 5 (or higher) PCF polynomials over QQ was found
