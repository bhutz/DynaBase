Postcritically Finite Maps

Dimension 1, degree 9 maps over ℚ

A map is postcritically finite (PCF) if every critical point has a finite forward orbit. Its critical portrait is the graph of the critical points and their forward orbits. Critical points are counted over the algebraic closure, including ∞ for polynomials.
