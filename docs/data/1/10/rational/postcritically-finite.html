Dimension 1, Degree 10, Rational: Postcritically Finite Maps

A map is postcritically finite (PCF) if every critical point has a finite forward orbit. Its critical portrait is the graph of the critical points and their forward orbits. Critical points are counted over the algebraic closure, including ∞ for polynomials.

Polynomials are rational maps too, but they are left out here: the PCF polynomials of this degree are listed on the polynomial page.

No postcritically finite maps are in the database for this combination yet.
