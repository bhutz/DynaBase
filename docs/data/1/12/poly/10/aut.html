Automorphism Groups

Dimension 1, degree 12 polynomials over fields of degree 10

The automorphism group of a map is the group of changes of coordinates (elements of PGL2) that conjugate the map to itself, computed over the algebraic closure. Each nontrivial automorphism group is listed once, with the map of smallest height that has it. Groups are named as in GAP: Cn is cyclic of order n, Dn is dihedral of order 2n (D3 is written S3), and A4, S4 and A5 are the tetrahedral, octahedral and icosahedral groups.

No maps with a nontrivial automorphism group are in the database for this combination yet.
