Dimension 1, Degree 4, Polynomial: Automorphism Groups

The automorphism group of a map is the group of changes of coordinates (elements of PGL2) that conjugate the map to itself, computed over the algebraic closure. Only maps with a nontrivial automorphism group are listed. Groups are named as in GAP: Cn is cyclic of order n, Dn is dihedral of order 2n (D3 is written S3), and A4, S4 and A5 are the tetrahedral, octahedral and icosahedral groups.
